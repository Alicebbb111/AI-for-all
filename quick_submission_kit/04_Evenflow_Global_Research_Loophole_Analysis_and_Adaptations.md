## 1. Executive Synthesis: Why Focusing 100% on the Depot Dispatcher (`นายท่า`) Is the Most Realistic Path in Thailand

A critical mistake in many smart-mobility hackathon proposals is trying to solve bus bunching (`รถเมล์มาพร้อมกันเป็นก้อนแล้วขาดระยะ`) by building another consumer passenger app or installing thousands of smart bus-stop kiosks across Bangkok. Auditing real deployments in Thailand and globally proves why **targeting the Depot Dispatcher (`นายท่าปล่อยรถที่อู่`)** is far more effective, realistic, and immediately deployable:
1. **Zero Consumer Behavior Dependency:** Passenger apps only show where the bus is *after* it has already bunched; they cannot fix the departure spacing at the depot. By equipping the **Depot Dispatcher (`นายท่า`)** with real-time AI departure scheduling, **every passenger on the route automatically gets more regular buses (`รถไม่ขาดระยะ`) and less crowded cabins (`ลดความหนาแน่นคนบนรถ`) without a single passenger needing to open an app!**
2. **Zero Street-Infrastructure Bureaucracy:** Installing hardware at public bus stops requires Bangkok Metropolitan Administration (BMA) sidewalk permits, power grid hookups (`฿100k–500k+/stop`), and faces narrow `< 1.5m` footpath constraints `[source: BMA Smart Bus Shelter reports]`. Operating **100% inside the bus depot (`ห้องนายท่า`)** requires **zero street permits**—a fleet operator (`ขสมก.`, `Thai Smile Bus`, or `Campus Shuttle`) can turn it on in their depot control room on Day 1.
3. **Proven by Our Own 840-Run Digital Twin (`experiments/optimization-summary.json`):** When we tested mid-route bus holding (`กักรถที่ป้ายกลางทางขณะมีผู้โดยสารนั่งอยู่บนรถ`) vs. **Depot Dispatch Optimization (`ปรับจังหวะเวลาปล่อยรถจากอู่ต้นสาย`)** across `840` SHA-256 hash-verified simulation runs, **mid-route holding actually hurt Total Passenger Time by `+652.83 person-seconds`**, whereas **Depot Dispatch Optimization saved `-660.00 person-seconds` across `30 / 30` disrupted seeds (`100%` win rate) and reduced corridor headway variance by `-3.3%` with `0%` onboard passenger harm!**

## 3. Solving the "What If All Buses Are Stuck in Bangkok Traffic?" Question (Zero-"เอ๊ะ" Turnaround Logic)

A sharp judge or experienced depot manager (`นายท่า`) will ask:
> *"ถ้าวันนั้นฝนตกหนักหรือรถติดทั้งเส้นจนรถเมล์ขาเข้ากลับมาถึงอู่ช้าหมด แล้ว AI จะเอาตารางที่ไหนไปสั่งปล่อยรถให้ไม่ขาดระยะ?"*

Evenflow handles this exact real-world Thai depot scenario through **3 Practical Depot Dispatch Modes** built into our logic:
1. **Mode 1 — Proactive Pre-Gap Pacing (`ชะลอจังหวะปล่อยรถที่เหลือในอู่ล่วงหน้าเพื่อเกลี่ยช่องไฟ`):**  
   Because Evenflow tracks inbound buses returning toward the depot **20–30 minutes before arrival** using 728-point OSRM road speeds and live rain/signal delays, the AI sees an upcoming turnaround shortage *before* the depot runs out of buses. Instead of releasing the last 2 buses in the depot at `15-minute` intervals and then suffering a **`45-minute` total blackout (`รถขาดระยะยาว`)**, the AI recommends pacing the remaining depot buses at **`22-minute` intervals (`+7 min` shift)**—cutting maximum passenger wait variance by over `30%` and preventing a single crush-loaded bus from taking the entire crowd alone.
2. **Mode 2 — Early Release When Edge-AI Camera Shows High Crowding & Bay Has Ready Buses (`ปล่อยรถเร็วขึ้น -1 ถึง -3 นาทีเมื่อกล้อง AI พบคนแน่นและมีรถพร้อมในอู่`):**  
   When the depot bay has a rested driver (`≥ 6 min`) and ready vehicle (`EV SoC ≥ 35%`) while downstream buses report **90° Edge-AI camera crowding `> 85%`**, the AI recommends releasing the next bus `1–3 minutes` early to share the passenger load.
3. **Mode 3 — Reserve Bus Injection Alert (`แจ้งเตือนนายท่าดึงรถสำรองเมื่อวิกฤตเกินเกณฑ์`):**  
   When inbound delays exceed the pacing buffer, the AI explicitly alerts the dispatcher (`นายท่า`) how many minutes of gap will occur unless 1 depot reserve vehicle (`รถสำรองในอู่`) is inserted at a specific timestamp—giving the human dispatcher crystal-clear situational awareness.
